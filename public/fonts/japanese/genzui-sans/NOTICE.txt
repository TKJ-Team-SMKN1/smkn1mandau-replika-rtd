GenZui Sans / 源萃ゴシック

Noto Sans JP Regular and Bold: Japanese base, kana components and transcription symbols.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp
Noto Sans Hentaigana: 286 hentaigana from instances at weight axis 410 (Regular)
and 770 (Bold), calibrated against Noto Sans JP; four archaic kana from the
Regular instance and an instance at axis 780 (Bold).
The upstream unencoded E and YE drawings receive U+1B000 and U+1B001.
https://github.com/notofonts/hentaigana
GenSeki Hentaigana Gothic 1.201 Regular and Bold: 20 historical kana, small kana and ligatures.
GenZui refits KOTO, alternate NE and small archaic YE to their kana peers.
Its source projects include GenSeki Gothic, Sukima Gothic and Shokaki Hentaigana Gothic.
https://github.com/MihailJP/GenSekiHentaiganaGothic
Noto Sans CJK JP Regular and Bold: U+5344 (卄), converted to TrueType curves.
https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
FRB Taiwanese Kana by Fredrick R. Brennan: Minnan tone letters and combining marks.
Bold blends them toward GenZui Bold masters drawn on the same points.
https://github.com/ctrlcctrlv/FRBTaiwaneseKana
GenZui: alternate WI, transcription symbols, SQUARE PAATU and layout.
